OneVoice State Priorities

Bills are matched by keywords in their title, subjects and full bill text. A keyword in the title counts on its own; in the bill text, a priority's keywords must appear at least 3 times. A match means a bill is worth a look, not that it supports or opposes the agenda. Use Edit keywords to tune what each priority catches.
