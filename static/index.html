                Hearings in your Outlook calendar

                Subscribe once and every committee meeting with a OneVoice, tracked or Relevant bill appears in Outlook (or Google or iPhone calendar) with the agenda link and the bills being heard. Canceled meetings update themselves. Outlook refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours.

                How to add it to Outlook
                1. Tap Copy above.
                2. Outlook on the web or new Outlook: open Calendar, click Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
                3. Paste the link, name it “OneVoice hearings”, and click Import.
                4. On an iPhone: Settings › Calendar › Accounts › Add Account › Other › Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste the link.

                Anyone with this link can see the hearing calendar (nothing else). If it gets shared too widely, make a new link; the old one stops working.
